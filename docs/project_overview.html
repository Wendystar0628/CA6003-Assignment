CA6003 · NHANES
概览

项目概览

在一个基础、可解释的糖尿病模型里，数据准备和标签选择会改变什么？一个很高的 AUC，是否真的意味着模型找到了尚未确诊的病例？

数据与队列

5,730成年人，NHANES 2021 年 8 月至 2023 年 8 月，16 个公开文件
971阳性：自报诊断或 HbA1c ≥ 6.5 %（未加权 16.9 %，加权 14.0 %）
152尚未确诊的阳性，位于 4,911 人的筛查人群 E-SCREEN
1,146留出集（20 %），冻结后只评估一次

评估两次：E-ALL 是全部成年人，E-SCREEN 只含尚未确诊者。模型固定为 L2 逻辑回归加样条，另用深度 3 的决策树做探针。

流程

三种准备方式共用同样的折、同样的模型，差别只在数据准备。M0 是带泄漏的对照，M1 是“最小”基线，S 是 14 步系统化方案。

主要发现

−0.131泄漏闸门：E-ALL 上 AUC 从 0.961 降到 0.828，区间 [−0.148, −0.114]。去掉治疗、确诊史、血糖检测项，多出来的那部分是泄漏。
+0.055识别差距：S 在全体成人上 AUC 0.836，在尚未确诊者上 0.781，区间 [+0.028, +0.086]。整体 AUC 部分是在认出已确诊者。
+0.019就医痕迹项：去掉两个“医生告知”题，E-ALL 的 AUC 降 0.016，E-SCREEN 反升 0.019，区间 [+0.004, +0.034]。
30.5 %标签是测量：819 名自报者中 250 人 HbA1c 低于 6.5 %，其中 165 人在用药。

最小准备对系统准备

+0.008S 减 M1 的 AUC（E-ALL），区间 [0.000, +0.016]
0.011最小可检测差，大于 0.01 的等效边界
−0.002去掉类别权重后 Brier 的差（S−B6a 对 M1）；S 对 M1 的 Brier 代价 +0.074 全部来自类别权重
结论是 inconclusive（不确定）：AUC 差异的区间碰到 0，且样本只能检出大于 0.01 的差，所以这是检验力不足，不能写成“两者没差别”。审计后用码本重编码的 M1c、收敛的 M1、嵌套选 C 重算，结论不变。系统化准备带来的是数据保真和（经由标准化的）系数稳定，不是更高的区分度。

特色

  • 把标签当作测量：自报、HbA1c、合并三种标签并排，另建 3×3 分层。
  • 同一个模型在“全体成人”和“尚未确诊者”两个人群上各评一次。
  • 14 个准备步骤逐个可开关，按六个块做消融；假设用 SHA-256 在实验前锁定，留出集只评估一次。
  • 只用基础可解释模型，机器学习是证据，不是目标。
  • 治理材料齐全：26 条论断账本、17 条局限、决策登记和 GenAI 日志；外部评审提出的 6 个问题已在锁定后更正，没有改变任何锁定结论。

数据准备与评估方法

同一个基础模型，只改变数据准备和标签的做法，看哪些决定真正改变结果。本页依次讲样本、标签、三个对照臂、模型、评估规则和治理方法。

标签与评估人群

训练标签 L_comb 是“自报医生诊断 或 HbA1c ≥ 6.5 %”。971 个阳性里 84.3 % 早已被诊断，所以全体人群上的高 AUC 可能只是认出了已诊断的人。因此 E-ALL 和 E-SCREEN 并列为主要评估人群。

上：971 个阳性由三部分构成，L_self 与 L_A1c 在“已诊断，未控制”处重叠。下：E-SCREEN 是 E-ALL 去掉已自报诊断者，阳性只剩 152 个，所以很多 E-SCREEN 结论预期是 inconclusive。

3×3 标签矩阵把同一个想法放到公平性分析里：用三种标签训练，看每种标签漏掉谁。

H3 在看结果之前写下的预测：自报标签漏掉多数未诊断者，仅用 HbA1c 的标签漏掉多数已控制的已诊断者，组合标签的最差格差距最小。结果见结果页。

三个对照臂

三个臂用同一个模型和同一批折，只差数据准备。每一步都包在可开关的封装里，全部关闭等于 M0，这一点在 §6.10 用独立写的朴素流水线断言过。

M0 朴素（Tier 1 共 88 列）

表里能用的列全部喂给模型，包括不该用的：血糖、降糖治疗、确诊年龄、“被告知糖尿病前期”、“做过血糖检测”。不修 XPT 零值，缺失码 7/9/77/99 整体替换。

M1 朴素最小（46 列）

只做 P03 可用性规则，去掉不该用的列。仍保留整体 7/9 替换，也不做其他准备。是最低限度的基线。

S 系统（42 列）

P01 到 P13 全开，分在 B1 到 B6 六个块里。比 M1 更窄，因为聚合和剪枝去掉了冗余列，虽然新增了样条、指示变量和标记。

绿点：步骤开启。橙色空心圈：步骤关闭，但朴素做法仍在起作用（P02 整体替换缺失码，P10 普通独热）。S 只开 P01 到 P13；P14（腰高比代替腰围）只在 S+WHtR 臂中开启。

每个块还有一个“去掉该块”的臂（S−B1 到 S−B6b），用来看这个块在其他步骤都在时的贡献。步骤分为三类：七个无状态，五个只在训练折的特征上拟合，两个作用在估计器上。

M1c （审计后新增）：M1 加上 P02，即按码本逐变量处理缺失码。S − M1c 在 E-ALL 上的 AUC 差为 +0.007 [−0.001, +0.014]，结论仍是 inconclusive，与 S − M1 相同，所以文中把 M1 称为“朴素最小”。

模型

L2 逻辑回归 + B 样条

主模型。年龄和 BMI 用三次 B 样条（4 个结点；Tier 2 再加 HDL 和总胆固醇），因为 §4 的非线性检验显示它们明显弯曲。正则强度 C0 = 0.1 在 S 臂训练部分上用内层 CV 选一次，之后冻结，所有臂共用。Tier 1 每个参数约 18.5 个阳性事件。

深度 3 决策树（探针）

最小叶 50。它不是竞争模型，而是探针：在 M0 的列上第一次分裂是否落在泄漏变量上（B2t），换成 M1 的列后是否改为合法变量。

为什么用基础模型

  • 课程要求只用基础、可解释的模型：机器学习在这里是证据，不是目标。
  • 问题是准备和标签的决定改变了什么。模型固定，差异才能归到数据上。
  • 系数可以读：共线性、符号翻转、泄漏都看得见；树的第一次分裂直接暴露泄漏。
  • 每个建模选择都对应一个 §4 的诊断数字，并说明为什么不选另一种。

评估协议

E-ALL 取校正重采样 t 与配对 bootstrap 两个区间中较宽的一个。所有臂在相同的折上比较，所以配对差异里没有拆分噪声。E-SCREEN 每折只有约 24 个阳性，只用 bootstrap，按合并后的折外预测计算；E-ALL 与 E-SCREEN 用同一批预测，只是评分的行和标签不同。

裁决词汇

最小关注效应量 SESOI：ΔAUC 0.01，ΔPR-AUC 0.02，ΔBrier 0.005，系数 SD 比 2，组间 TPR 差 0.05。

这是方向性假设的读法（src/evalkit.py 的 verdict）。等效、非劣效和 SD 比值各有自己的锁定规则，写在 hypotheses.md 的“Operational verdict rules”里。

预注册与留出集

假设在任何实验结果之前锁定：hypotheses.md 的 SHA-256 是 de032f4d…610c，对应标签 prereg-lock，共 17 条假设。内部留出集在 exp-freeze 之后只评估一次，评估前有一道检查：C0、折哈希、留出集哈希和五个建模文件必须与冻结时一致。留出集不是独立验证，因为全队列的 §4、§5 诊断先于拆分。

治理方法

NCHS 数据使用协议（DUA）

5 项义务逐条对应到做法并勾选：只做统计分析；不识别个人；不与其他个人级数据链接；不研究或破解披露控制；发现身份即上报。个人级数据行不交给 GenAI 工具。

数据最小化

16 个文件共 175 列，保留 78 列，删除 97 列（55 %）：准标识符、军役、家庭细节、无用的 MCQ 项。每个删除列都写明理由，加载后立即丢弃。

调查权重与折刀法

HbA1c 队列用 WTPH2YR。15 个层、30 个 PSU，删一个 PSU 的折刀法，全国自由度 15。加权患病率 14.0 %，未加权 16.9 %；Kish 设计效应 1.55。模型主结果不加权，权重放在独立的 P12 / B6b。

NCHS 呈现规则

样本量或有效样本量 < 30、CI 宽度 ≥ 0.30、域自由度 < 8 的估计一律标为探索性；比例用 Korn-Graubard 区间。

结果

结果图表

每张图都按已存储的结果文件逐点绘制，图下一句话说明该看什么。所有区间为 95 %，SESOI 为 ±0.01 AUC。

E-ALL = 全部 5,730 名成人，971 例阳性。 E-SCREEN = 尚未确诊的 4,911 人，152 例阳性（筛查场景）。

两个人群的 AUC

每个模型在全体成人上的 AUC 都高于在尚未确诊者上的 AUC。S 的差距为 +0.055，95 % 区间 [+0.028, +0.086]。去掉两个“医生告知”题目后差距缩到 0.020：整体 AUC 的一部分来自认出已确诊者。点为 5×5 交叉验证的汇总 AUC，未画单点区间。

泄漏闸门

E-ALL 上 AUC 从 0.961 降到 0.828，E-SCREEN 上降幅较小（−0.072），说明泄漏项也在帮模型认出已确诊者。这个下降是正确的：被去掉的是模型在真实筛查时拿不到的信息。

  • 可用性规则剔除 22 个一级特征候选，包括糖尿病用药（DIQ070）、确诊年龄（DID040）和血糖检测史。
  • M0 的深度 3 决策树根节点是 DIQ070，第二层用 DID040；确诊年龄 86 % 缺失，被中位数填成常数 50。
  • 在 E-SCREEN 上，M0 的树 AUC 反而低于 M1（0.633 对 0.701）。

关键对比

S 与 M1 的差只有 +0.008，区间下端贴近 0、上端略超 0.01，最小可检测差 0.011 大于 SESOI，结论是 inconclusive（检验力不足，不等于没有差别）。换成码本重编码的 M1c、去掉类别权重的对照，区间同样跨过带的边界。就医痕迹项的方向在两个人群里相反：E-ALL 上去掉后 AUC 下降，E-SCREEN 上反而上升。

标签测量

重叠数 569 由 819 + 721 − 971 算出，与混淆表一致。自报阳性里有 250 人（30.5 %）HbA1c 低于 6.5 %，其中 165 人在用药，血糖被药物压下去了，所以“标签”本身是一种测量，不是真值。未参保者的未确诊份额比参保者高 23.2 个百分点；未参保组只有 49 名 HbA1c 阳性者，这个比例本身只作探索性报告。

留出集与交叉验证

留出集 0.851 / 0.791，交叉验证 0.836 / 0.781（E-ALL / E-SCREEN）。方向和大小一致，留出集区间都盖住交叉验证的点。它是内部留出检查，不是独立验证：部分留出行参与过全队列的描述性诊断（审计后更正）。

假设记分板

17 个假设中 10 个成立，1 个部分成立（B3），6 个不确定，没有被整体否定；审计前后假设层面的计数相同。子论断层面，审计把两条标准差比值的结论撤回为“不可检验”（B3-b 和 H2b-c），其余不变。“成立”多数来自已看过数据的假设；真正事先登记、没看过数据的假设有 6 个，其中 3 个成立、3 个不确定。

整体重编码的代价

1,496个有效数值被“7 / 9 / 77 / 99 = 缺失”的整体重编码抹掉（队列内，20 个候选变量）
29个 HbA1c 阳性值丢失，占 721 个中的 4.0 %（HbA1c 恰好为 7.0 与 9.0 被当成哨兵值）
6个合并标签翻转（合并标签共 971 例阳性）

损失集中在血压和脉搏读数（真实读数恰好等于 77、99 这类哨兵码）。最要紧的是 HbA1c 自己丢了 29 个阳性值，因为标签由它定义；好在最后只翻转了 6 个合并标签，因为这些人多数同时自报了糖尿病。

Overview

Project Overview

In a basic, interpretable diabetes model, what do data-preparation and labelling decisions change, and does a high AUC mean the model finds people who are not yet diagnosed?

Data and cohort

5,730adults, NHANES Aug 2021 to Aug 2023, from 16 public files
971positives: self-report or HbA1c ≥ 6.5 % (16.9 % unweighted, 14.0 % weighted)
152positives not yet diagnosed, in the 4,911-person screening group E-SCREEN
1,146hold-out (20 %), evaluated once after the freeze

Every model is scored twice: on E-ALL, all adults, and on E-SCREEN, only adults not yet diagnosed. The model is fixed as L2 logistic regression with splines, plus a depth-3 tree as a probe.

Pipeline

The three preparation arms share the same folds and the same models, so only the preparation differs. M0 is the leaking control, M1 the minimal baseline, S the 14-step systematic arm.

Headline findings

−0.131Leak gate: AUC on E-ALL falls from 0.961 to 0.828, interval [−0.148, −0.114]. Removing treatment, diagnosis-history and glucose-testing items shows that the excess was leakage.
+0.055Recognition gap: S scores 0.836 on all adults but 0.781 on adults not yet diagnosed, interval [+0.028, +0.086]. A whole-sample AUC partly measures recognising known cases.
+0.019Care traces: dropping the two told-by-a-doctor items lowers E-ALL AUC by 0.016 but raises E-SCREEN AUC by 0.019, interval [+0.004, +0.034].
30.5 %Labels are measurements: 250 of 819 self-reported cases have HbA1c below 6.5 %, and 165 of them take medication.

Minimal versus systematic

+0.008AUC of S minus M1 on E-ALL, interval [0.000, +0.016]
0.011minimal detectable difference, above the 0.01 equivalence margin
−0.002Brier difference without class weights (S−B6a vs M1); the S vs M1 Brier cost of +0.074 comes from class weights alone
The verdict is inconclusive: the interval for the AUC difference touches 0 and the data can only detect differences above 0.01, so this is low power, not proof of no difference. Re-running with a codebook-recoded M1 (M1c), a converged M1 and a nested C changed nothing. Systematic preparation buys data fidelity and, through scaling, stable coefficients; it does not buy discrimination.

What is different

  • The label is treated as a measurement: self-report, HbA1c and their union are compared, with a 3×3 strata matrix.
  • The same model is scored on all adults and on adults not yet diagnosed.
  • Each of the 14 preparation steps is a switch, ablated in six blocks; hypotheses are locked by SHA-256 before any experiment and the hold-out is scored once.
  • Only basic, interpretable models are used; machine learning is evidence, not the goal.
  • Governance artefacts are complete: a ledger of 26 claims, 17 limitations, a decision register and a GenAI log. Six issues from an external review were corrected after the lock without changing any locked verdict.

Method

Preparation and evaluation method

One basic model, with only the data preparation and the label changed, to see which decisions change the result. This page covers the cohort, the labels, the three arms, the models, the evaluation rules and the governance methods.

Cohort funnel

Bars are drawn to scale; grid lines at 5,000 and 10,000. The 5,730 adults are split at random, stratified by label × race, into 4,584 training rows and a 1,146-row internal hold-out.

5,730analysis cohort: adults, examined, HbA1c present, not pregnant
4,584training part
1,146internal hold-out
16.9 %L_comb prevalence, unweighted (971 positives); 14.0 % weighted

Labels and populations

The training label L_comb is “self-reported physician diagnosis OR HbA1c ≥ 6.5 %”. Of the 971 positives, 84.3 % were already diagnosed, so a high AUC on everyone may only mean that the model recognises diagnosed people. E-ALL and E-SCREEN are therefore co-primary populations.

Top: the 971 positives have three parts; L_self and L_A1c overlap in “diagnosed, uncontrolled”. Bottom: E-SCREEN is E-ALL without the self-reported cases and keeps only 152 positives, so many E-SCREEN verdicts are expected to be inconclusive.

The 3×3 label matrix carries the same idea into the fairness analysis: train on each of three labels and see who each one misses.

H3 was written before any result: the self-report label misses most undiagnosed cases, the HbA1c-only label misses most controlled diagnosed cases, and the combined label has the smallest worst-cell gap. Results are on the Results page.

Three arms

The three arms use the same model and the same folds and differ only in preparation. Every step sits behind a switch; all off reproduces M0, which §6.10 asserts against an independently written naive pipeline.

M0 naive (88 Tier 1 columns)

Every usable column goes in, including inadmissible ones: glucose, diabetes treatment, age at diagnosis, “told prediabetes”, “glucose tested”. XPT zeros are not repaired and the codes 7/9/77/99 are recoded everywhere.

M1 naive-minimal (46 columns)

Only the P03 availability rule: inadmissible columns are removed. It keeps the blanket 7/9 recode and does no other preparation. It is the minimum-effort baseline.

S systematic (42 columns)

P01 to P13 on, in six blocks B1 to B6. It is narrower than M1 because aggregation and pruning remove redundant columns, even though splines, indicators and flags are added.

Green dot: step on. Orange ring: step off, but the naive alternative acts (P02 blanket recode, P10 plain one-hot). S has P01 to P13 on; P14 (waist-to-height ratio instead of waist) is on only in the S+WHtR arm.

Each block also has a leave-one-block-out arm (S−B1 to S−B6b), which shows what the block adds when every other step is present. Seven steps are stateless, five are fitted on the training fold features only, and two act on the estimator.

M1c (added after the audit): M1 plus P02, the per-variable codebook recode. S − M1c on E-ALL is +0.007 [−0.001, +0.014], still inconclusive, the same reading as S − M1. This is why M1 is called naive-minimal.

Models

L2 logistic regression + B-splines

The main model. Age and BMI get cubic B-splines (4 knots; Tier 2 adds HDL and total cholesterol) because the §4 tests show clear curvature. The penalty C0 = 0.1 is chosen once by inner CV on the S arm's training part, then frozen for every arm. Tier 1 has about 18.5 events per parameter.

Depth-3 decision tree (probe)

Minimum leaf 50. It is a probe, not a competitor: does the first split on the M0 columns fall on a leakage variable (B2t), and does it move to an admissible variable on the M1 columns?

Why basic models

  • The brief allows only basic, interpretable models: machine learning is evidence here, not the goal.
  • The question is what preparation and label decisions change. With the model held fixed, a difference can be traced to the data.
  • Coefficients can be read: collinearity, sign flips and leakage are visible, and the first split of a tree exposes leakage.
  • Each modelling choice is tied to a §4 diagnostic number, with the reason for not choosing the alternative.

Evaluation protocol

For E-ALL the wider of the corrected resampled t interval and the paired bootstrap interval is used. All arms are scored on identical folds, so paired differences carry no split noise. E-SCREEN has about 24 positives per fold, so it uses the bootstrap only, on the pooled out-of-fold predictions; E-ALL and E-SCREEN share one set of predictions and differ only in the scored rows and label.

Verdict vocabulary

Smallest effect sizes of interest (SESOI): ΔAUC 0.01, ΔPR-AUC 0.02, ΔBrier 0.005, coefficient SD ratio 2, group TPR gap 0.05.

This is the reading for a directional claim (verdict in src/evalkit.py). Equivalence, non-inferiority and SD-ratio claims have their own locked rules in hypotheses.md under “Operational verdict rules”.

Pre-registration and hold-out

Hypotheses were locked before any experimental result: the SHA-256 of hypotheses.md is de032f4d…610c (tag prereg-lock), 17 hypotheses in all. The internal hold-out is scored once, after exp-freeze, behind a gate that checks C0, the fold hash, the hold-out hash and five modelling files against the frozen values. It is not an independent validation, because the §4 and §5 diagnostics used the full cohort before the split existed.

Governance methods

NCHS Data User Agreement (DUA)

5 obligations are each mapped to a practice and ticked: statistical use only; no re-identification; no linkage with other individual-level data; no study of the disclosure controls; report any identity found. No participant-level rows go to GenAI tools.

Minimisation

Of 175 columns in 16 files, 78 are kept and 97 dropped (55 %): quasi-identifiers, military service, household details, unrelated MCQ items. Each dropped column has a stated reason and is discarded right after reading.

Survey weights and jackknife

WTPH2YR is the weight for the HbA1c cohort. 15 strata and 30 PSUs, delete-one-PSU jackknife, national df 15. Weighted prevalence is 14.0 % against 16.9 % unweighted; Kish design effect 1.55. Model results are unweighted; weights sit in the separate P12 / B6b step.

NCHS presentation rules

Estimates with n or effective n below 30, CI width of 0.30 or more, or domain df below 8 are labelled exploratory. Proportions use Korn-Graubard intervals.

Results

Results Charts

Every chart is drawn point by point from the stored result files, with one sentence on what to take from it. All intervals are 95 %; the SESOI is ±0.01 AUC.

E-ALL = all 5,730 adults, 971 positives. E-SCREEN = the 4,911 adults not yet diagnosed, 152 positives (the screening setting).

AUC by population

Every arm scores higher on all adults than on adults not yet diagnosed. For S the gap is +0.055, 95 % interval [+0.028, +0.086]. Without the two told-by-a-doctor items the gap shrinks to 0.020: part of the whole-sample AUC is recognition of known cases. Dots are pooled 5×5 CV AUC; per-point intervals are not drawn.

Leak gate

AUC falls from 0.961 to 0.828 on E-ALL; the fall is smaller on E-SCREEN (−0.072), so the leaking items also helped recognise known cases. The fall is a correct one: what is removed is information a real screening tool would not have.

  • The availability rule drops 22 Tier 1 candidates, including diabetes pills (DIQ070), age at diagnosis (DID040) and glucose-testing history.
  • The M0 depth-3 tree splits first on DIQ070, then on DID040; age at diagnosis is 86 % missing and the median fill turns it into the constant 50.
  • On E-SCREEN the M0 tree is worse than the M1 tree (0.633 vs 0.701).

Key contrasts

S beats M1 by only +0.008; the interval touches 0 and crosses 0.01, and the minimal detectable difference 0.011 exceeds the SESOI, so the verdict is inconclusive (underpowered, not proof of no difference). Replacing M1 with M1c (codebook recode) or dropping class weights gives intervals that cross the band edge as well. The care items point in opposite directions in the two populations: removing them lowers AUC on E-ALL and raises it on E-SCREEN.

Label measurement

The overlap 569 is 819 + 721 − 971, matching the confusion table. 250 self-reported cases (30.5 %) have HbA1c below 6.5 %, and 165 of them take medication, which holds glucose down; so a label is a measurement, not the truth. The undiagnosed share is 23.2 points higher for uninsured adults; that group has only 49 adults with HbA1c ≥ 6.5 %, so the share itself is exploratory.

Hold-out versus CV

Hold-out 0.851 / 0.791, cross-validation 0.836 / 0.781 (E-ALL / E-SCREEN). Same direction and size, and each hold-out interval covers the CV estimate. It is an internal hold-out check, not independent validation: some hold-out rows took part in full-cohort descriptive diagnostics (corrected post-audit).

Hypothesis scoreboard

Of 17 hypotheses, 10 are supported, 1 is mixed (B3), 6 are inconclusive and none is refuted overall; the hypothesis-level counts are the same before and after the post-audit correction. At sub-claim level the audit withdrew two standard-deviation-ratio verdicts as not testable (B3-b and H2b-c); nothing else moved. Most 'supported' verdicts belong to hypotheses written after the data had been seen; the 6 hypotheses with no prior exposure split 3 supported and 3 inconclusive.

Blanket recode damage

1,496valid values wiped by a blanket “7 / 9 / 77 / 99 = missing” recode (cohort, 20 candidate variables)
29HbA1c-positive values lost, 4.0 % of 721 (HbA1c of exactly 7.0 and 9.0 read as sentinel codes)

Losses concentrate in blood pressure and pulse readings, whose genuine readings can equal sentinel codes such as 77 and 99. The costly one is HbA1c itself: 29 positive values lost, because the label is defined from it. Only 6 combined labels flipped in the end, since most of those people also self-reported diabetes.
